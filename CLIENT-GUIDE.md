# Your website: a simple guide

## Changing anything on the website
1. Go to **bearingmind.com/studio** and sign in.
2. Choose what to change:
   - **Home page:** every section has its own tab (Top, About, Sessions, Questions, Research notes…).
   - **Sessions & booking:** fees, durations, days and times, the concern list, booking page wording, UPI ID.
   - **Website settings:** phone, email, address, menu, and text for Google.
3. Edit, then click **Publish** (bottom right). The website updates within a minute.

## Writing a blog post or article
1. In the Studio, open **Blog** or **Articles** and click the **+** button.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**
   - **Web address**: click **Generate**
   - **Short summary**: two or three sentences
   - **Main image**: add a short description of the image
   - **Text**: use **Heading** for section titles
3. Choose yourself as the **Author**, and add **Sources** if you quote studies.
4. If you like, add 2–4 **Questions and answers** at the end. Google and AI assistants often quote these.
5. Click **Publish**.

## Your bookings
- Open the **BearingMind Bookings** Google Sheet. The Google Sheets app works on your phone too.
- **Confirmed** means paid and verified. Nothing to check. The session is already in your Google Calendar, and online sessions have their Google Meet link.
- **Payment to verify** appears only while online payment is not yet switched on. Check the UTR in your bank app, then set Status to **Confirmed**.
- For a cancellation or reschedule, change Status to **Rescheduled** or **Cancelled**. The time opens again on the website and the calendar event is removed.
- For dining sessions, send the hotel details to the patient after confirmation.
- **Paid – needs attention** means the patient paid but needs a call from you. The email explains why.

The patient's main concern and notes are only in the Sheet. They are never sent by email, for privacy.
